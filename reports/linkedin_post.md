I built a Whole Foods dashboard from Amazon’s public 10-K — store sales, 365, and Prime — the way I would walk a merchant through the banner.

What I wanted to see:

• Store sales from $17.2B (2018) to $22.6B (2025) and $23.4B annualized in 2026 YTD. Whole Foods is now Amazon’s only physical grocery banner.

• 365 as the house brand (the Kirkland equivalent): 24.8% of grocery, $57 vs $77 for national Whole Foods brands on the same 12-item basket, next to Kroger, still above Trader Joe’s.

• Prime’s effect on the store: Prime members are about 54% of Whole Foods sales, up from about a third in 2018. Pickup and delivery are 20% of the store — and Amazon books those trips in Online stores, not in the Physical stores line.

The filings are public. Category mix, Prime share, and state sales are modeled and labeled. Happy to walk through the pages.
