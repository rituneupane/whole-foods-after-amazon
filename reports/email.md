Sharing a short independent brief I put together on Whole Foods since the Amazon acquisition.

I used Amazon’s 10-K Physical stores line (Whole Foods is nearly all of it) from 2018 through August 2026, then built three pages a merchant would actually open:

1. Overview — store sales, category mix, and sales by state
2. 365 — the house brand vs a 12-item peer basket
3. Prime’s effect — Prime members’ share of the store, and pickup vs delivery

A few takeaways:

• Store sales are about 36% above 2018 ($17.2B → $23.4B annualized 2026 YTD). Whole Foods is now Amazon’s only physical grocery banner.
• 365 is 24.8% of grocery. A 12-item 365 basket is $57 vs $77 for national Whole Foods brands, next to Kroger ($58).
• Prime members are about 54% of Whole Foods sales (about a third in 2018). Pickup and delivery are 20% of the store.
